# 总问题定义

Routing on the interposer of FPIA consists in assigning routing resources to each net of the network in order to connect its driver to each of its loads. The routing resources in the interposer and their connections are represented by the directed graph $G=(V, E)$. The set of vertices V corresponds to the bumps, horizontal lines, vertical lines and tracks. The set of edges E corresponds to the switches between bumps and horizontal lines, horizontal lines and vertical lines, vertical lines and tracks and neighbouring tracks assigned to the same COBUnit. When given a set of drivers(sources) ,a set of loads(sinks) and the structure of the interposer as input, the router should decide the status of all the switches such that the vertices are connected by these switches and forming many pathes, by which the loads are connected with its driver. the status of all the switches are the output of this problem, and also the variables. The objective is minimizing the wire length(calculated by the total numbers of bumps & tracks in all pathes). There are several constraints: 1) connectivity constraint: loads should be connected to its dirver, and the path should be constructed by consecutive vertices. 2) exclusivity constraint: two pathes connecting different (driver, load1, load2, ...) must not share the same vertice  3) bus syncronization constraint: those 2-pin net in the same bus should share the same wirelength
